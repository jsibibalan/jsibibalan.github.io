Thanks for stopping by!  I am Sibi (short for Sibibalan Jeevanandam), a PhD candidate in Dynamic Systems and Controls at the School of Mechanical Engineering at Purdue University. I am advised by <a href="https://jain-research-lab.github.io/">Prof. Neera Jain</a>. I expect to graduate in Summer 2027.

The goal of my dissertation is to enable <a href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-driving_car#automation-levels">conditionally automated vehicles</a> to be aware of the human driver they interact with. A human-aware vehicle could use estimates of its user's cognitive states (e.g., trust and stress levels) to adjust its behavior (e.g., amount of information displayed to the user) to improve safety. 

Toward this goal, my graduate research lies at the intersection of hybrid dynamical systems, state estimation, set-based methods, and human-machine interaction. I have built <a href="https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/abstract/document/11423829">low-order hybrid models</a> of human cognitive states and automation reliance during conditionally automated driving, and <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.02308">set-based estimators</a> and <a href="https://skoge.folk.ntnu.no/prost/proceedings/ifac2026_IFACWC26_preprints/files/3197.pdf">sensor-scheduling algorithms</a> that predict them without probabilistic assumptions about the human. I have also worked on enabling set-based robust control co-design to guarantee safety for a nonlinear thermal management system under unknown but bounded heat loads.
