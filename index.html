Sibibalan Jeevanandam

Purdue University | sjeevana [at] purdue [dot] edu

Thanks for stopping by! I am Sibi (short for Sibibalan Jeevanandam), a PhD candidate in Dynamic Systems and Controls at the School of Mechanical Engineering at Purdue University. I am advised by Prof. Neera Jain. I expect to graduate in Summer 2027.

My graduate research lies at the intersection of hybrid dynamical systems, state estimation, set-based methods, and human-machine interaction. I have built low-order hybrid models of human cognitive states and automation reliance during conditionally automated vehicles, and set-based estimators and sensor-scheduling algorithms that predict them without probabilistic assumptions about the human. I have also worked on enabling set-based robust control co-design to guarantee safety for a nonlinear thermal management system under unknown but bounded heat loads.

Outside research, I serve as the Vice-President of the Herrick Student Resource Committee, have mentored undergraduates through Purdue’s SURF program, and co-chaired the 2025 ICON Student Research Conference.

Outside of graduate school, you can find me being an amateur at road cycling, gymming, running, or playing my bass guitar.
